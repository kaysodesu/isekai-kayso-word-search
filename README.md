# Seek & Find: Word Search by Isekai Kayso Studio

**Play it here:** https://kaysodesu.github.io/isekai-kayso-word-search/

175 free word search puzzles for PC, tablet and phone. Pick how much time you have, follow an anime-style storybook adventure, earn Grotto Coins, and dress up your chibi Seeker.

## Features

- **175 puzzles:** 35 themes, each at five lengths: Speedrun (1 min), Coffee Break (5), Lunch Break (10), One Episode (15) and Binge Mode (30). Themes range from anime and manga to 90s throwbacks, Y2K internet, food, travel and the seasons.
- **The Scattered Storybook:** a short comic plays as you start each theme. Finish all five of its puzzles to restore that page of the story.
- **Unlock as you go:** in each theme, the 5-minute puzzle unlocks after the 1-minute one, then 10, 15 and 30.
- **Grotto Coins:** earn 1–5 coins per puzzle, depending on your time, plus a bonus for each finished story. Spend them on power-ups (Spark, Spyglass, Time Freeze), buffers (Time Buffer, Coin Shield) and outfits.
- **Beat the clock:** finish a 10, 15 or 30-minute puzzle within its time goal for **double coins**, and unlock the achievements *Lunch Break Legend*, *Skip Intro Energy* and *Binge Boss* on your profile.
- **Chibi closet:**
  - Feminine, masculine and non-binary body styles.
  - Skin tones, eye colors, hair colors and 7 hairstyles.
  - Hair accessories, shirts, pants and shoes, with 15 outfit slots.
- **Leaderboard:** your personal hall of fame, with fastest solves at every length and your totals.
- **Comfort:** dark mode, three text sizes and a reduce-motion option.
- **Works everywhere:** mouse, touch or pen. Drag across a word, or tap its first and last letter.

## Your privacy

Everything is saved **only in your own browser**, on your device. There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing is sent to any server.

To keep your progress safe or move it to another device, use **Leaderboard → Download backup**, then **Restore backup** on the other device. Clearing your browser's site data erases your progress.
